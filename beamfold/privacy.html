Beamfold Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026. Updated 15 November 2026 to cover the website.

Short version: Beamfold does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything stays on your device.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Beamfold puzzle game for Android (package name com.beamfold.app), published on Google Play, and to the Beamfold daily puzzle website.

Information we collect

None. Beamfold has no servers and does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else. The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no third-party trackers, and it does not use the Android advertising ID.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, Beamfold saves the following in the app's private storage on your device:

  • which puzzles you have solved, with your stars and tap counts;
  • your Daily Beam results and streak;
  • your settings (sound, vibration, colour letters).

This data never leaves your device through Beamfold. If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Google may include app data in your personal backup under your Google account's terms; Beamfold has no access to that backup.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used for the light tick you feel when a mirror turns. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Internet: required by the Android component that displays the game. Beamfold itself makes no network requests and works fully offline.

Sharing your result

If you tap "Share result", Beamfold hands a short text (your Daily Beam score) to Android's share sheet. You choose where it goes. Beamfold does not see or record what you share or with whom.

The website

The daily puzzle at pyraco.github.io/beamfold-site runs entirely in your browser. It saves your results, streak and settings in your browser's local storage on your own device, and sends nothing to us. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.

The pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when your browser requests the pages, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive those logs.

Children

Beamfold does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in the game and choose "Reset all progress", or uninstall the app. Either removes everything Beamfold has stored.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open a request at github.com/Pyraco/beamfold-site/issues, or use the developer contact email shown on Beamfold's Google Play listing.
